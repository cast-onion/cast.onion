# Station Reporting Policy

**Last Updated:** March 28, 2026

This policy explains how users may report stations on cast.onion and what information is required for review.

---

## 1. When You Can Report a Station

You may report a station if it is used for:

### Copyright Infringement

* Streaming or distributing copyrighted audio without authorization
* Unauthorized rebroadcasting of protected content

### Illegal Content

* Any content that violates applicable laws

### Abuse or Harassment

* Hate speech, threats, or targeted harassment
* Content intended to harm or intimidate users

### Security Violations

* Attempted exploitation of the Service
* Malicious streams, payloads, or disruptions

### Spam or Platform Abuse

* Automated or repetitive unwanted broadcasts
* Attempts to disrupt normal service operation

### Impersonation

* Pretending to be another user, brand, or station without authorization

## 3. Required Information

To properly investigate a report, you must include:

* Station name
* Station key or identifier (if available)
* Description of the issue
* Time and date of the incident (if applicable)
* Evidence (screenshots, recordings, or logs if available)
* Your contact information

Incomplete reports may delay or prevent investigation.

---

## 4. Review Process

Upon receiving a report, we may:

* Review the station activity and metadata
* Temporarily restrict or suspend station access
* Request additional information from the reporter
* Take enforcement action, including revocation of station privileges

We are not required to notify users of reporting outcomes.

---

## 5. False or Abusive Reports

Submitting knowingly false or malicious reports may result in:

* Suspension of reporting privileges
* Restriction or termination of Service access

---

## 6. Enforcement Authority

We reserve the right to:

* Investigate reported stations at our discretion
* Suspend, restrict, or revoke any station at any time
* Take action without prior notice if necessary to protect the Service
